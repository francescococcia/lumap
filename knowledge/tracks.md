# Tracks (from https://gb-community-eng-hackathon-10-2026.teamdeel.workers.dev/hackathon#tracks)

Every track comes from a real problem a London community lead sent. **Everything must be open source**: public GitHub repo with an MIT, Apache-2.0 or AGPL-3.0 licence file. Code freeze confirmed 21:50.

1. **Gatekeeper** (vetting & moderation) - review applications, keep scammers out, share a blocklist across groups.
2. **Know Your Members** (members, intros & speakers) - find out who your members are, introduce them to each other, find speakers among them.
3. **Event Autopilot** (run sheet, promo & newsletter) - turn one event into a run sheet, posts, visuals and a newsletter a lead approves with one click.
4. **Open Venue & Events DB** - open database of London venues, communities and events that approved organisers keep up to date together.
5. **Front Desk & Revenue** (inbound, pricing, sponsors) - answer inbound in chat, price bookings fairly, find sponsors.
6. **Weird Stuff** (wildcard) - your own community's problem that doesn't fit the others.

## Updated judging criteria (official site)
- Technical execution
- Is it open source, and could another community run it next week?
- Use of community context
- Better than what leads use today
- Real-world usefulness
- Demo quality
