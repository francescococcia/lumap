# Judging

Every team submits a working demo at code freeze. Top 5 present live, 3 minutes each.

## Criteria
1. Technical execution
2. How much Grok Bot and Cursor architected it
3. Use of community context
4. 10x-20x improvement over today's tooling
5. Real-world usefulness
6. Demo quality

## How to win this room
- Most judges run communities: solve a pain they recognise (cross-posting events across Luma/Discord/WhatsApp, matching members between communities, activation).
- Prove the 10x with a before/after (minutes or clicks today vs with the tool).
- Make the Grok Bot + Cursor workflow visible: Grok Bot holding and acting on community context; show Cursor plans and commits.
- Supabase underneath, Cognee as memory: both free tonight and their contacts are in the room.
- Demo one moment flawlessly; record a backup video before freeze.
